Profile

I specialize in the people behind the algorithms.

As a seasoned recruitment specialist based in Hong Kong, I partner with organizations to scale their Fintech, Quant, and AI teams. Whether it’s sourcing mission-critical developers, cutting-edge AI innovators, or elite quantitative minds, I focus on finding the technical precision required to drive modern financial and technology growth.
